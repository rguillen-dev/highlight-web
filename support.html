Where are my events stored?

Only on your devices: your iPhone or iPad, and your paired Apple Watch. There is no account and no cloud copy. Deleting Highlight deletes its events, and reinstalling starts empty. Restoring your device from a backup made before you deleted the app brings them back.

Will I lose my events when I update?

No. Updates keep all your events.

How do I get Highlight on my Apple Watch?

Highlight 1.1 and later include a watch app that works together with the iPhone app. On your iPhone, open the Watch app, find Highlight under Available Apps and tap Install.
